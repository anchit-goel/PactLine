# Design system: soft-glass bento (black and white)

Tokens live in `app/globals.css` (`:root`). Light theme only.
Canvas `#F4F4F2`, ink `#0B0B0C`, muted `#71717A`, semantic success/danger/warn only, chart series ink / `#A1A1AA` / `#D4D4D8`.
Radii: panel 28, card 24, row 16, chip 12, pill 999. Card padding 24, page padding 16, grid gap 20.
Type: Plus Jakarta Sans (next/font) with a system fallback; greeting 44/48, KPI 40, title 17, body 14.
Full guide: `docs/FRONTEND.md`.
