# Product

## Users
Brand managers, creator managers and finance/legal reviewers who turn a deal brief into a cited contract and a payout schedule.

## Purpose
Show an agentic pipeline end to end: retrieval, contract drafting with source citations, compliance review, human approval gates and sandboxed milestone maths. Decision support, not legal advice. Demo tax rates are unverified.

## Principles
- Never show a number that did not come from the API. Label stub and replay data "simulated".
- Quiet, light, legible: colour only where it carries meaning (success, warning, danger).
- Every approval is an explicit human action.
